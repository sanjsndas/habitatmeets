    This policy explains how Habitat Meets Form uses cookies and similar technologies — and how you can manage them.

    Last updated: January 15, 2025

    Habitat Meets Form ("we," "us," or "our") operates habitatmeetsform.com (the "Site"). This Cookies Policy describes how we use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies when you visit the Site, and how you can control them.

    This policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle personal information more broadly.

    In short: We use a small number of cookies to make the Site work properly, understand which articles are read, and remember your preferences. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

    1. What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, tablet, or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to site owners about how visitors interact with their content.

    Cookies do not contain programs and cannot carry viruses. They do not give us access to your device or to any personal information beyond what you voluntarily share with us.

    Other tracking technologies

    In addition to cookies, we may use:

    • Web beacons (pixel tags): Tiny transparent images embedded in pages or emails that help us understand whether content was viewed.
    • Local storage: Browser storage that remembers settings or preferences without sending data back to a server.
    • Analytics scripts: JavaScript snippets that collect aggregated, anonymous usage statistics.

    2. How We Use Cookies

    We use cookies for the following purposes:

    • Site operation: Ensuring the Site loads correctly, functions smoothly, and remains secure.
    • Analytics: Understanding which articles and topics are most popular, how visitors navigate the Site, and where we can improve.
    • Preferences: Remembering choices you make, such as your preferred category filter or search settings.
    • Performance: Monitoring page load times and identifying technical issues.

    We do not use cookies to serve targeted advertising, and we do not sell data collected through cookies.

    3. Types of Cookies We Use

    Cookies can be classified by their duration and by their origin:

    By duration

    • Session cookies: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser. They help maintain your session as you navigate the Site.
    • Persistent cookies: Cookies that remain on your device for a set period (e.g., 12 months) or until you delete them. They remember your preferences for future visits.

    By origin

    • First-party cookies: Set by Habitat Meets Form directly. These are primarily used for site functionality and preferences.
    • Third-party cookies: Set by external services we use, such as Google Analytics, to help us understand site traffic. These are governed by the respective third-party privacy policies.

    4. Specific Cookies We Use

    The table below describes the main cookies we use and their purposes. Cookie names and exact durations may change as we update our systems.

    Cookie Name Type Purpose Duration
    hmf_session Essential (1st party) Maintains your session and keeps the Site functioning as you navigate between pages. Session
    hmf_prefs Preference (1st party) Remembers your category filter and display preferences. 12 months
    _ga Analytics (3rd party — Google) Used by Google Analytics to distinguish unique users and measure site traffic. 24 months
    _gid Analytics (3rd party — Google) Used by Google Analytics to store and update a unique value for each page visited. 24 hours
    hmf_consent Essential (1st party) Stores your cookie consent preference so we do not ask repeatedly. 12 months

    Third-party cookies (such as _ga and _gid) are placed by Google Analytics. We use the anonymized, aggregated data these cookies provide to understand readership patterns. You can learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and opt out via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

    5. Your Cookie Choices

    You have several options for managing cookies:

    Browser settings

    Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

    • View what cookies are stored and delete individual ones
    • Block third-party cookies
    • Block all cookies from specific sites
    • Clear all cookies when you close your browser

    Browser help pages provide specific instructions:

    • Google Chrome
    • Mozilla Firefox
    • Safari
    • Microsoft Edge

    Opt-out tools

    For analytics cookies specifically, you can use:

    • The Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
    • The Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page (for participating third parties)
    • The Your Online Choices tool (EU/UK)

    Please note: If you block or delete essential cookies, some parts of the Site (such as your saved preferences or session state) may not function properly.

    6. Do Not Track Signals

    Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) setting that signals your preference not to be tracked across websites. Because there is no industry-wide standard for how to interpret DNT signals, our Site does not currently respond to DNT requests. However, you can use the cookie controls described above to limit tracking.

    7. Cookies and Personal Data

    Most cookies we use collect anonymous, aggregated information. Where a cookie could be linked to personal data (for example, if you contact us and we associate your inquiry with analytics data), we handle that information in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

    We do not use cookies to build profiles for advertising purposes, and we do not share cookie data with advertisers.

    8. Changes to This Cookies Policy

    We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or our practices. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you via a notice on the Site.

    We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we use cookies.
