    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the best way to start an interior design project? +

    Start with function, not aesthetics. List how you use the space, your storage needs, and your daily routines. Then define a budget and gather inspiration. A clear brief will save you time and money.

    How much does a home renovation typically cost? +

    Costs vary widely by region and scope. A minor cosmetic refresh might be $150–$300 per square meter, while a full gut renovation can exceed $1,500 per square meter. Always add a 15–20% contingency.

    Do I need an architect for a small renovation? +

    Not always. For cosmetic updates, a designer or contractor can suffice. But if you're moving walls, changing structure, or altering the building envelope, an architect ensures safety and compliance.

    What's the difference between interior design and interior architecture? +

    Interior design focuses on finishes, furniture, and styling. Interior architecture deals with the structural and spatial elements — walls, openings, staircases, and built-ins. The lines often blur in practice.

    How long does a typical renovation take? +

    A bathroom might take 4–6 weeks; a kitchen 8–12 weeks; a full home renovation 6–12 months. Permits and material lead times are the biggest variables.
